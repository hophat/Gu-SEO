/ TECHNOLOGY

One pipeline.
Four autonomous stages.

The engine ingests a brand profile, plans the next post, writes it, ships it, and notifies search engines — all without a human in the loop. Each stage is a standalone service; together they are a production line.

Keyword discovery

Scrapes Google Autocomplete, parses competitor sitemaps, scores intent — and dedups against everything already shipped. The brief a writer gets is never a duplicate of what came before.

Multi-agent authoring

A planner, a writer, and a fact-checker run as parallel agents. The output is 900–1,300 words structured for search — with FAQ schema, internal links, and source citations baked in.

Visual generation

Workers AI generates on-brand cover imagery per article. SVG for the brand mark, WebP for hero shots, OpenGraph variants for social. No design team, no stock photo subscriptions.

IndexNow broadcast

Every published URL is pushed to Bing and Yandex the same second. Search engines learn about new content before the next human wakes up.

Content refresh

Articles older than 90 days are automatically scanned, refreshed against new data, and re-published at the same URL. Rankings that decay are re-earned.

Programmatic pages

Service-by-location, category-by-attribute, any structured dataset — generates hundreds of pages from a single CSV, each unique to its query.
